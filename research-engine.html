Distributed computational network

Nine repositories share one versioned evidence contract. Eight consumer workflows execute independently, validate the public registry, and publish commit-linked integration receipts. The native scientific engines remain in their source repositories.

Topology and authority

Radar publishes the shared contract and dataset provenance. E47-Kartekeya supplies the canonical spectral construction; Time, Genesis, Foundry, Electroweak, Bismuth and other repositories retain their domain-specific computation. The website presents read-only state.
