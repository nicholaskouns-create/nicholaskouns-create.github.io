Instruments

Working instruments from the Mathematical City, the open research collection of Recursive Intelligence. Most of them study one exact object, E47: the 47-dimensional kernel of the selector K = (C − 6I)(C − 30I) on the 125-dimensional space of three spin-2 systems. Each runs in your browser at its own address.

Mathematics

Kartekeya SPEC 001

The specification, Revision A, for Kartekeya: a spectral coprocessor built around E47. A Richardson filter Γ = I − εK2 carries any state onto the 47-dimensional sector at the exact rate (15/17)n; bench pages cover the Casimir ledger, Q4.28 fixed-point arithmetic, the PQSPI handshake and the certificates.

SCALAR

Algebraic quantum scalar fields. The 125-to-47 contraction is lifted to a field obeying (L − 6)(L − 30)φ = 0, with L = −Δ; step the contraction, lock onto E47, compare the full field with its transverse residual, and read the Casimir shells and a seven-check audit.

SPECTRA

The quadratic selector K = (C − λ1I)(C − λ2I) on matrices you choose: cycle, path, complete, band, star or random GOE, of order 4 to 12. Lock λ1 and λ2 to the spectrum of C and read the kernel’s dimension, rank, trace and residual as they change.

BUILD

A kinetic contraction lab. Each step moves every point a fraction α of the way to the centroid μ, so the spread falls exactly as Dk = (1 − α)2kD0; place, drag and delete points, try the presets, and watch a theorem audit check the closed form.

Murmuration

A topological view of the research corpus itself. Records from Notion, GitHub, Drive, Python and Supabase become a point cloud you can orbit, scale and tap, with a filtration ε, a contraction control and the H0 persistence count β0 read out live.

Fun With Einstein

Three exact results, side by side. The five-cycle’s two nonzero Laplacian eigenvalues stand in the ratio φ−2; a quantum filter on E47 fires with probability 47/(125φ2) = 0.1436…; and a scalar–tensor model reduces to Einstein’s equation when its scalar is stationary.

Eigen Explorer

Eigenvectors you can drag. Move an input vector and edit the four entries of a 2 × 2 matrix; the mapped vector, the real eigendirections and the vector field update together, with a bridge onward to the 125-dimensional Casimir filter.

Source

Protein folding

InvariFoldE2

A protein-folding cinema for chains of up to 80 residues. Start from Trp-cage, CLN025, Villin HP-35 or Ala-14; mutate residues, force helix, strand or coil, pin contacts, run alanine scans and Trp-cage knockouts, and export each run as JSON, CSV or PDB-style JSON. The paper sets out the method and carries the same lab.

Paper

Levinthal’s Paradox: Spectral DepthE0 E1 E3

Levinthal counted configurations; this instrument counts steps. It sets the blind-search time bNτ0 against a depth law in which the step count depends on the landscape’s spectral spread and never on the number of configurations, folds a model chain from a random coil, and compares the result with measured folding times for CLN025, Trp-cage and Villin HP-35.

Quantum

The Quantum ClockE0 E1 E2

A qubit held inside E47 that reads its own proper time. Its hand is the phase between the C = 6 and C = 30 sectors; it keeps time exactly through leak and recovery, clocks on different worldlines drift apart by exactly (N1 − N2)ω, and 48 precision checks are recomputed in your browser.

Source

E47 Φ4 Quantum Circuit Atlas

From invariant to quantum circuit: exact QSP synthesis of the degree-4 filter Q4, Clebsch–Gordan transport, and a two-ancilla LCU, in 125, 250 and 500 dimensions on a nine-qubit register. Step through the gate chain and each Casimir sector; the fourteen-check certificate reruns in Python.

Source

The Matrix

A three-qubit statevector laboratory. Place gates or load the Bell, GHZ and QFT-like presets, run the circuit, read all eight complex amplitudes and each qubit’s reduced state, and hand the result to other instruments as a SHA-256 receipt.

Source

Superposition Lab

A five-step classroom on superposition: a coin that has not landed, a probability mixer, a hundred measurements counted live, a first circuit in The Matrix, and a three-question check.

Source

The Electron’s Secret Garden

Quantum waves inside bismuth, for newcomers. Make a twelve-atom chain regular, alternating or disordered, then step through its electron states and see where each wave lives, all from an exactly diagonalized tight-binding model that runs offline.

Monorail Operations Guide

How the Elevated Reproducibility Monorail runs: theorem files compiled in Lean 4.35 and Coq 8.18, each result sealed in a proof passport and chained by SHA-256 receipts, with its trust boundaries, a twenty-threat model, an append-only correction switchyard and human-gated promotion. It states plainly what is deployed and what is not.

The gateway for machine certificates. A payload without a transit passport, or with a hash not on the roll, is refused; the rest pass the K filter, and each becomes a proof-bound citizen. Five sample payloads show both outcomes.

Infrastructure and records

GRID

The U.S. power grid, live from public sources including EIA-930, the ISO/RTO operators, NOAA, NWS, USGS, NIFC and NASA, on night-light, satellite and 3D terrain maps you can fly from your own position. County outage alerts, water systems and hazards sit beside a certified spectral auditor that refuses a corrupted grid topology.

Source

SEE: An AI to Mirror You

A personal mirror built only from evidence you choose to give it. Add statements with their sources, then ask: answers stay bound to those records, tensions between them are flagged, and “unknown” comes back where the evidence is silent. An optional language model runs on your device, with no account or API key.

Source

Mnemosyne

A local-first research archive. Record an artifact with its evidence status, seal it with a SHA-256 receipt, export or import the archive, and sync across devices by email code if you choose. It is also the way into SPECTRA, the proof explainers, the E47 Forge and SEE.
