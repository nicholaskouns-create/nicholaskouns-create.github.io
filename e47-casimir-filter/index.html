Unitary construction: seven data qubits (125 of 128 basis states), one signal ancilla, and one LCU ancilla form a 512-dimensional Hilbert space. The upper-left signal block of the standard dilation is P(A). An outer Hadamard–SELECT(U,U†)–Hadamard yields Q₄(A) on selecting ancillas |00⟩. This is a dense reference dilation, not a compiled low-depth QSP gate sequence. Each stage succeeds probabilistically under ancilla postselection; the plotted Qⁿ is the unnormalized selected operator, not a deterministic channel.

Evidence: exact polynomial identities and a locally executed NumPy 125×125 sector certificate. The full 512×512 Qiskit/QuTiP circuit was not executed as part of this publication. The rank-47 finite polynomial is an exact algebraic identity, not an asserted elementary-gate implementation.
