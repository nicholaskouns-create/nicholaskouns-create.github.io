WHY IT HAPPENS

Retention is not a clock hand

Protected-sector retention measures Tr(P₄₇ρ). Elapsed time is inferred from relative phase between protected Casimir sectors. One number measures where probability weight resides; the other measures how phase evolved.

Δτ = Δφ / ω   (for fixed angular frequency)

The reference comparison provides a concrete case where these rankings disagree.

HOW THE INSTRUMENTS FIT

One kernel, four instruments, one fork

Every clock in this programme is mounted inside the same completed chamber: V₂⊗V₂⊗V₂ → C → K=(C−6I)(C−30I) → ker K = E₄₇. They differ in where on E₄₇ they stand, and Prime Clock §7 named that difference before any of them was built.

The representation-covariance fork. A generic 47-cycle may mix the 5V₂ and 2V₅ sectors and need not commute with the diagonal SU(2) action. Two designs follow, and they are not interchangeable:
Branch A · full 47-state clock. Exact order 47, one transitive cyclic orbit, 47 is the kernel dimension itself — but basis-dependent and not SU(2)-covariant.
Branch B · representation-preserving clock. Acts on the multiplicity spaces, commutes with SU(2), genuinely protected under collective noise — but does not naturally form a single 47-state orbit.
Stated in the source as an open design choice, not a cosmetic difference.

Prime Clock's canonicity warning: E₄₇ canonically determines the clock dimension, but not the cyclic basis. A unique clock requires an additional rule selecting the basis, its ordering, a phase origin, and a readout convention. The logical simulator supplies all four — basis r = a + 5b + 25c, ordering by r, origin the flat state on r = 0…46, readout by Fourier overlap. It is a concrete answer to that open question, standing on the other branch.

THE LOOP THAT CLOSES

Two flows, one projector

Prime Clock §3 locks the kernel by contraction: limt→∞ e−tK² = P₄₇, equivalently limn→∞ (I − εK²)n = P₄₇. Taking the trace of that limit against the maximally mixed state gives a scalar retention curve

R(t) = (1/125) Σj dj · e−2[(λj−6)(λj−30)]²t,   R(0) = 1,  R(∞) = 47/125 = Ωc

Exactly two of the seven Casimir sectors have zero rate — λ=6 and λ=30, carrying 25 and 22 — so the limit is the kernel fraction and nothing else. Ωc is the asymptotic survival fraction of the contraction flow, a dynamical characterisation of 47/125 distinct from the kernel-dimension one.

tr K = 0 is the moment-selector identity restated: K = (C−μ)² − σ² with μ = 18, σ = 12, so its normalised trace vanishes by construction. The same two eigenvalues — the gap and the maximum — set the continuous time constant τ = 1/23,328 and the discrete optimal step ε*.

Boundary — two different flows. The 99.02% figures on this page are not R(t). They measure a state prepared inside E₄₇ leaking out under the engine's Lindblad model over six seconds. R(t) measures the K² contraction pulling the maximally mixed state into E₄₇ from outside. Both converge on P₄₇; they are different dynamics with different initial conditions, and the shared projector is not a shared result.
THE LUMEN RESULT

The matched clock is an exact one-qubit memory

Nathan Dickerson and Lumen (ChatGPT) analyzed the E₄₇ clock comparison. Lumen reproduced the six-second timing discrepancy and explained why matched leak-and-recovery dynamics preserve the encoded qubit up to its intended clock rotation. The detuning-matched clock in the engine is named lumen for that analysis; matched is retained as an alias.

The four-state node is one logical qubit × one protected-or-leaked flag, and the recovery operator carries no logical index. When the two branch detunings agree — the Lumen condition, Δa = Δb — then H₁ = H₀ + ωI exactly, so H = IL⊗h + ω nL⊗I and both that term and R act trivially on the logical factor. Every logical block obeys dρll′/dt = Λ(ρll′) − iω(l−l′)ρll′ with the same flag superoperator Λ. The encoded state stays a product with the flag and comes back exactly once the known rotation is undone.

Tested the sharp way — storing half of an entangled pair, since a channel can preserve every single-qubit state and still destroy entanglement. ω = 2, c = 1, γ = 20, perfect Bell negativity = 0.5:

The inversion, at the level of stored information. Linear retains more E₄₇ weight — 0.990256 against 0.990220 — and is the branch losing the entanglement. The mechanism is exact and it is in the engine's own closed form: leak = 4c²/(γ² + 8c² + 4δ²). δ² sits in the denominator, so detuning a branch further off resonance suppresses its leakage — and that same detuning asymmetry is precisely what dephases the logical qubit. One knob drives retention up and memory down. Higher retention, worse time, worse memory.

Verified twice, independently: tests/memory.test.js against the engine's own linear algebra, and a separate reconstruction of H and R in Python. Both agree to every digit shown. Retention matches the closed form to 10⁻⁶ in both clocks. This is a result about the model — Hamiltonian realisation, tick duration and hardware remain uncertified, as the Prime Clock page lists.
