What does it mean?

An ordered crystal has a regular repeating atomic pattern. Some of its modeled electronic states extend across many sites.

A THING TO REMEMBER

A peak is not a trapped electron.

Density of states and localization measure different properties. A high density of states does not by itself imply superconductivity.

Original scientific plots

These graphs are recomputed from research/bismuth_heterostructure/run.py: a 48-site, four-state-per-site Hamiltonian, seed 83, with Gaussian DOS broadening σ = 0.065. The right chart shows the twelve eigenstates nearest zero energy per model. They are model proxies, not experimental spectra.

Blue: bulk A7 Bi proxy. Orange: ideal Bi/Mg superlattice proxy. Green: disordered Bi-Pb / Mg-Zn proxy. Energy is in model units.

Reproduce the browser dataset with python app/export_model.py. Within degenerate subspaces, eigenvectors and individual-state IPR can vary across numerical libraries; these charts use one reproducible exported basis. The animation maps each of the 48 model sites to patches of a conceptual 2D lattice. Selected eigenstates use their exported site weights; the energy dial controls an illustrative surface when no state is selected. The underlying Hamiltonian is a 1D chain. It does not reproduce the model geometry or depict electron trajectories.
